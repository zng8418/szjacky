szjacky - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to szjacky ("we", "us", or "our"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application that integrates with the Pinterest platform.

2. Information We Collect

Our application accesses the following Pinterest data through the official Pinterest API:

  • Pin creation and scheduling data
  • Board and publishing content information
  • Account analytics and performance data

We do not collect any additional personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the application's functionality.

3. How We Use Information

The information accessed through the Pinterest API is used solely to:

  • Create and schedule Pins on behalf of the authorized account
  • Publish content to Pinterest boards
  • Retrieve account performance analytics

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is processed in real-time and is not permanently stored on our servers. OAuth tokens are encrypted and stored securely. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect any data in transit.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. Data is only shared with Pinterest as required by the API integration.

6. User Rights

You may revoke the application's access to your Pinterest account at any time through your Pinterest account settings. Upon revocation, all stored tokens are deleted.
